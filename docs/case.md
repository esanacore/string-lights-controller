Generated by `hardware/case/uno_case.py` (Python, `manifold3d` + `trimesh`). Edit the PARAMETERS block and run `python uno_case.py` to regenerate all three STLs.

No supports needed.

## Print settings (PETG, Adventurer 5M Pro, 0.6 mm nozzle)

0.3 mm layers, 4 walls, 4 top/bottom, 20% gyroid, about 240 °C nozzle / 80 °C bed.

## Features

- **Base:** 4 standoffs for M3 self-tappers at the Uno R3 hole pattern; cutouts for USB-B and the barrel jack sized for the cable plug overmold; open-top slot at the far end for the string leads.
- **Lid:** friction-fit lip (0.25 mm per side), notch matching the wire slot, vent slots.
- **Matrix window:** 31 mm opening with a 0.8 mm lip; a 32.6 mm pocket frame underneath takes the LED block from inside. Hot glue in place, wires pointing away from the USB end.
- **S1 plunger:** guide tube at S1 (25.5 mm from the USB end, 3.75 mm from the power-header edge, both measured with calipers). The plunger drops in from inside, collar down; the collar rests on S1 and about 3 mm of rod sticks up through the lid.
- Lid features are mirrored in Y in the model, because the lid prints face down and flips onto the base.

## Design history

1. 41.6 mm base, sized for the shield with its stick-on mini breadboard.
2. 35.6 mm after deciding to solder parts to the shield instead.
3. Wire hole changed to an open-top slot (the string can't be threaded through a closed hole).
4. 49.6 mm base, lid with matrix frame and S1 plunger tube.
5. S1 tube moved from an estimated 9 mm to the measured 3.75 mm from the power-header edge; tube OD reduced to 8.8 mm to clear the wall.

## Assembly

1. Glue the matrix into the lid frame, wires away from the USB end.
2. Drop the plunger into its tube from inside, collar end down.
3. Screw the Uno into the base (USB and jack to their cutouts).
4. Lay the string leads in the slot with a loose knot inside for strain relief.
5. Fold the matrix leads in, align the lid notch with the slot, press on. Check the plunger clicks S1.

## Unverified
