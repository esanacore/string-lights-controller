# Case

> v1 (`uno_case.py`, `stl_v1/`) is printed and in use. v2 (`uno_case_v2.py`, `stl_v2/`) is designed and reviewed; base and plugs are sliced, lid waits on module measurements. v2 details are at the bottom.

Generated by `hardware/case/uno_case.py` (Python, `manifold3d` + `trimesh`). Edit the PARAMETERS block and run `python uno_case.py` to regenerate all three STLs.

## Print settings (PETG, Adventurer 5M Pro, 0.6 mm nozzle)

0.3 mm layers, 4 walls, 4 top/bottom, 20% gyroid, about 240 °C nozzle / 80 °C bed.

## Features

- **Base:** 4 standoffs for M3 self-tappers at the Uno R3 hole pattern; cutouts for USB-B and the barrel jack sized for the cable plug overmold; open-top slot at the far end for the string leads.
- **Lid:** friction-fit lip (0.25 mm per side), notch matching the wire slot, vent slots.
- **Matrix window:** 31 mm opening with a 0.8 mm lip; a 32.6 mm pocket frame underneath takes the LED block from inside. Hot glue in place, wires pointing away from the USB end.
- **S1 plunger:** guide tube at S1 (25.5 mm from the USB end, 3.75 mm from the power-header edge, both measured with calipers). The plunger drops in from inside, collar down; the collar rests on S1 and about 3 mm of rod sticks up through the lid.
- Lid features are mirrored in Y in the model, because the lid prints face down and flips onto the base.

## Design history

1. 41.6 mm base, sized for the shield with its stick-on mini breadboard.
2. 35.6 mm after deciding to solder parts to the shield instead.
3. Wire hole changed to an open-top slot (the string can't be threaded through a closed hole).
4. 49.6 mm base, lid with matrix frame and S1 plunger tube.
5. S1 tube moved from an estimated 9 mm to the measured 3.75 mm from the power-header edge; tube OD reduced to 8.8 mm to clear the wall.

## Assembly

1. Glue the matrix into the lid frame, wires away from the USB end.
2. Drop the plunger into its tube from inside, collar end down.
3. Screw the Uno into the base (USB and jack to their cutouts).
4. Lay the string leads in the slot with a loose knot inside for strain relief.
5. Fold the matrix leads in, align the lid notch with the slot, press on. Check the plunger clicks S1.

## Unverified

Not test-fit yet: S1 cap height (estimated 19.6 mm above the Uno PCB; the plunger has about 7 mm of extra travel), matrix clearance over the shield (about 4 mm planned), and connector cutout positions (from the standard Uno R3 drawing, visually matched to the RexQualis board). Print a 25 mm tall slice of the base first if you want a quick fit check.

# Case v2

## Why

v1 test fit (2026-11-16): window, barrel jack, lid seam and plunger all fit, but nothing retained the lid, the matrix or the board (all hot glued), the plunger bore needed drilling, and the USB cutout was ~6 mm off (v1 used y = 44.4, which is the USB-B connector's edge on the Uno drawing; the center is ~38.4). See photos `27-*`.

## What changed

| Area | v2 |
|---|---|
| USB cutout | Centered at y = 38.4, 16 mm wide |
| Lid retention | 4 corner bosses (7 mm, hanging from the rim with 45° gussets, no supports) with **M3x5** heat-set inserts; **M3x8 flat-head** screws countersunk flush in a 3 mm lid (7.0 mm countersink) |
| Board retention | Inserts in the standoffs: **M3x4 + M3x6 socket head** at the two far holes; **M2x4 + M2x6 socket head** at the two holes next to the barrel jack and the 10-pin header (an M3 head doesn't fit there and a wide standoff lands on solder tails) |
| Matrix retention | Two posts under the lid with **M2x3** inserts; **M2x6** screws through the HW-109 mounting holes pull the LED face against the window lip (posts end 0.3 mm short) |
| Plugs | Press-fit covers for the USB and barrel cutouts (0.1 mm/side, 0.3 mm at the top, chamfered corners, crush ribs, fingernail notch), and an H-section plug that slides down into the wire slot leaving a channel for the leads |
| Plunger bore | 6.8 mm with a 0.6 mm chamfered entry (v1 6.6, needed drilling) |
| Vents | Moved over the USB end; the matrix board now sits under the old vent area |
| Insert holes | M3 4.2 mm, M2 2.9 mm (0.6 nozzle prints holes ~0.2 mm small), 1 to 2 mm deeper than the insert |

All hardware is from stock: Kadrick inserts and tips, SZHKM/Vigrue socket heads, Comdox flat heads.

## Review

Before printing, the design was independently reviewed by a separate model run (DFM check with boolean/overhang analysis). Findings applied: M2 at the two crowded Uno holes (M3 head interfered ~0.5 mm with the header, standoff hit SCL solder tail), insert holes enlarged, M2 post OD 6.6, boss 8.5 to 7.0 mm (8.5 blocked the hex key at hole (66.1, 7.6)), countersink 6.4 to 7.0, rectangular plug fit reworked, wire-slot plug given an inner flange, lid lip cut over the slot widened to 10 mm, USB cutout back to 16 mm. Verified: single bodies, zero lid/base interference after the flip, lid holes aligned with boss holes, only overhangs are the two connector bridges, 45° gussets.

## Before printing the lid: measure the matrix module

The post positions are estimates. Measure on the HW-109 and set these in `uno_case_v2.py`:

1. `MX_FACE_TO_PCB`: LED face to the top surface of the green PCB (estimate 10.0).
2. `MX_HOLE_FROM_BLOCK` and `MX_HOLE_SPACING`: hole centers from the LED block's header-side edge (estimate 14.5) and between the two holes (estimate 26.0). Also the hole diameter.
3. How far the PCB and the soldered wires extend past the block on the header side (must clear the corner boss at x ≈ 62.6).

## Assembly (v2)

1. Press the inserts with the M3/M2 tips (soldering iron ~230 to 250 °C for PETG), flush or 0.2 mm proud, then let cool.
2. Uno into the base: tilt it in far end first (the bosses cover the PCB corners), then M2x6 at the two connector-end holes and M3x6 at the far two.
3. Lay the string leads in the slot channel, slide the slot plug down over them.
4. Matrix onto the lid posts with 2x M2x6. Plunger into its tube.
5. Lid on, 4x M3x8 flat head. Plugs into the USB or barrel cutouts you're not using.
