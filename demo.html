String Lights Controller

Live simulation of firmware v2.4. Click the round button (S1): short click toggles, hold about 0.7 s for the next effect. Same effects, icons and gamma as the Uno.

Apache-2.0. Source: firmware/string_lights/string_lights.ino. Hardware: Uno R3, 2N2222 on D9, MAX7219 on D10 to D12, S1 on D4.
